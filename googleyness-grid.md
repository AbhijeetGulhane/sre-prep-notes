## Verdict + the fix

**Conflict resolution is the gap to close.** Recommendation: **re-angle story #3 (AD migration)** to surface a conflict thread. Alternatively, if the FD-exhaustion outage (#1) involved disagreement about root cause or remediation under pressure, that's a natural second conflict story too.

**Action:** pick whichever of #3 or #1 had a *real* interpersonal conflict, and rehearse a version that leads with the conflict + resolution — so you have two independent conflict stories, not one.

Everything else is adequately covered. Don't add new stories — re-angle an existing one.
