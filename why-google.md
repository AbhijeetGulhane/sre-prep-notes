## The answer

"I've spent about eleven years in infrastructure — most recently as a Production Engineer at Barclays, where I run Linux at genuinely large scale, around sixty thousand nodes, and I'm on the hook when trading systems break. That RTB and incident work is where I found the part of the job I actually care about: the reliability side — why systems fail, how you make them not fail, and how you build the tooling so a human isn't the last line of defense.

Google is where that discipline was invented. SRE as a practice, the scale, the engineering rigor around reliability — it's the environment where the problems I like solving are the whole job, not a side effect. I want to go deeper into SRE at the place that defined it, and bring eleven years of running things under real pressure with me."

- **Ends on what you bring**, not just what you want — signals you're additive.
